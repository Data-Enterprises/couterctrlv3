What CounterCtrl Cloud is

Five modules. One set of definitions.

Most operators aren't short on data — they're short on agreement about what it means. POS says one thing, ERP another, DSD receiving a third. CounterCtrl connects to those systems, normalizes them against a single set of definitions, and surfaces the result across five modules, so a number means the same thing on every screen.

  • SalesStore, department and item performance, with this week, last week and last year built into every view.
  • Loss PreventionRegister activity analyzed at item and cashier level, surfacing patterns instead of listing exceptions.
  • OrdersWhat was ordered against what actually arrived, with the variance surfaced rather than discovered later.
  • Weekly PerformanceThe rhythm your team already runs — without the hours of assembly that precede the meeting.
  • CouponsRedemption reconciled against real unit movement, so a promotion's impact is visible rather than assumed.
How far it goes

From the whole week to a single receipt.

Start wide and keep going. Every view drills to the next level without a new report, a new export or a call to IT — and whatever you land on can be sent to the person who needs it.

A district manager opens the week, sees one store off pace, opens the store, sees one category, opens the category, sees the item — without leaving the screen or asking anyone to pull a report.

How access works

One platform that narrows or widens with the question.

CounterCtrl Cloud is multi-tenant at two levels: across companies, so no operator ever sees another operator's data, and within a company, so each person sees exactly the scope their role covers. A department manager opens their aisle. A district manager opens twelve stores. The office opens every banner.

Aisle→ Department→ Store→ District→ Banner

It is the same screen and the same definitions the whole way up, so nobody has to reconcile their version against anyone else's. As the question gets more complex, the platform adds the depth to answer it rather than handing you off to a different tool.

Getting your data in

Two ways in, depending on your POS.

CounterCtrl is built by DCR POS, so DCR sites get the deepest path: an integrated pipeline that feeds sales transactions, item and inventory, cashier metrics and operational data straight into the platform — live, with no manual step and no export to schedule.

If you run something else, that's fine. We already operate a range of ingestion pipelines and file formats for raw POS data, so the platform reads what your systems produce today rather than asking you to change them.

  • Integrated · DCRLive ingestion, zero-touch synchronization, built to scale.
  • Other POSEstablished pipelines and formats for raw POS data, running in production today.
  • Either wayNo new hardware in store, and no rip-and-replace of systems that already work.
Who we are

We come from the store, not the software industry.

DCR has sold and supported point of sale for 65 years — installing it, servicing it, and standing behind it in live stores. That is where CounterCtrl Cloud came from: not from watching retail from the outside, but from six decades of being in the aisle when the register goes down. Our customers needed to compete on decisions, not just on price — reliable data, and access to it when and where it matters most.

DCR POS has spent years inside regional grocery and retail operations — point of sale, back office, and the daily reality of running stores. CounterCtrl Cloud came out of that work rather than a whiteboard.

Software built for an industry from the outside tends to solve the version of the problem that looks clean in a slide deck. Software built alongside the people running stores gets shaped by the actual friction — mismatched report formats, numbers that don't agree, the exception list nobody has time to read.
