Add user no longer fills in the admin's own login

Fixed Dev tree. Prod gets it on promotion.
Found
Opening New on Users showed the test account's username and its saved password in the new user's fields.
Cause
Neither shared input passed autoComplete through to the field, so Chrome read the form as a sign-in and filled in whoever was signed in.
Files
components-dev/inputs/Input.tsx, PasswordInput.tsx, organization/dev/users/create/UserFields.tsx, organization/dev/users/view/SecurityTab.tsx

What changed

  • On Add user, username and email are autoComplete="off". Password and confirm are "new-password", which tells Chrome this is an account being created, not one signing in.
  • The Security tab's password fields get "new-password" too, since changing a password has the same risk.
  • Three tests pin the attribute so a later refactor can't drop it.

How to check it

  1. DEV mode, User Management, Users, press New, in a browser with a saved CounterCtrl password.
  2. Username, Email, Password and Confirm should all be empty. Chrome may offer to suggest a strong password, which is expected.
  3. Open a user, Security tab: the password fields should also be empty.
AN

A customer sees only their own company's tickets

Backstop in place Real fix is backend
Found
User 649 (Bruces, canViewAll: false) was shown 16 tickets: their own TCK-35 and 15 from company 1, opened by 3 other users.
Cause
Tickets have no company of their own. The server scopes a customer by "the opener shares a company with me", so a ticket opened by DCR staff who hold both Houchens and Bruces reaches customers at both. The page then showed whatever arrived; the list filter had no company check.
Files
pages/tickets/dev/ticketsUtils.ts (ticketsInScope), pages/tickets/dev/hooks.ts

What changed

  • New rule, applied before every tab and search: a customer sees tickets they opened, plus tickets filed under a company they hold. A ticket's company is the one the row reports, the opener's lowest company id, so a multi-company opener's ticket counts under one company instead of all of them.
  • Techs see everything, as before.
  • The tab counts read the same filtered list, so "Open" can't count tickets the customer will never see.
  • Five tests cover the review's exact case, a customer with no company, and the tech view.

This is a backstop in the page. The server still sends the other companies' tickets, and a ticket could still be opened by its address. The backend fix is to scope on the ticket's company in _SCOPE (api/routers/tickets.py), and properly by adding a tickets.company column. Both are in the backend plan.

How to check it

  1. Sign in as user 649 in DEV mode, Coming Soon, Tickets.

A

No suggested price for random-weight packs, and the page says why

Stop-gap Full fix needs the backend
Found
Ground Beef Fam Pak showed per-pound prices on the left ($3.49, $6.49) and 146 package totals on the right, then recommended "Moves fastest at $11.94 · 75.3% GM". Its real margin is about 24%.
Cause
The two tables decide "sold by weight" differently. The left reads the sales rows, which carry weight.   • Items weighed at the till, and each-priced items, are unchanged.

This stops a wrong number being acted on; it doesn't produce the right one. A per-pound price needs each receipt line's weight. Your backend branch already returns weight on transaction_list, but without the divide-by-1,000 rule topitems applies for company 4. Once that's in and deployed, the frontend can price these per pound and this notice goes.

How to check it

B G

"Moves fastest" needs history behind it, and the tree uses one money format

Fixed Two thresholds to tune, below.
Found
COKE's "moves fastest at $3.34" was 26 sales on one day, and $3.33 and $3.34 counted as two prices. Ground Beef's $11.94 was 11 packs on one day. The tree mixed "$25.1K", "$758.3" and "$674".
Cause
"Moves fastest" ranks by units a day while a price was live, so one busy day beats a price that sold all week. A multi-buy splits its total to the penny, so three for $10 rings as $3.33, $3.33, $3.34. The shared compact formatter keeps up to one decimal.
Files
utils/pricePoints.ts, ItemAnalysisPanel.tsx, InventoryTreePanel.tsx

What changed

  • A price can only be "moves fastest" if it rang on at least 3 days and 5 receipts. With fewer than two such prices there's no suggestion, and the panel says so: "Not enough history to suggest a price…".
  • Prices one cent apart under the same price type fold into one, kept at whichever price more receipts rang at. Nobody sets two shelf prices a cent apart.
  • The tree shows whole dollars under $1,000 and one decimal above: "$758", "$674", "$25.1K". This is local to Price Opt; the shared formatter is used by 23 other screens and is unchanged.

3 days and 5 receipts are starting values I picked, not measured ones. Three days is under half a week, so a price that stood all week qualifies easily and a one-day spike can't. They're named constants (MIN_SUGGESTION_DAYS, MIN_SUGGESTION_RECEIPTS) and worth tuning against real stores.

How to check it

  1. Price Opt, COKE 6PK: $3.33 and $3.34 are one row. If only one price has stood for 3 days, the grey "Not enough history" note shows instead of the blue strip.
  2. The department tree's amounts all read whole dollars or one decimal of a thousand.

A delivery at the end of the window isn't called dead

Fixed Board check now passes.
Found
