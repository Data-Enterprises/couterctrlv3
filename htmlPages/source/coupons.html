Coupons

See where coupons are being used by store, sub department, date or cashier, and open the transactions behind them.

Common questions
QHow do I load coupons?

Choose Store or Group, pick the store or group, set the date range and click Load Coupons. The button says "Select Store" or "Select Group" until you pick one.

QWhat does the store list on the left show?

Each store with its number of coupons and total coupon amount. For a group, "All stores" shows the whole group combined, and you can click a store to see just that store. Large groups also get a store search box.

QWhat does the Amt / Qty switch do?

It sorts the stores, groups and products by total coupon amount (Amt) or by number of coupons (Qty). The Date tab always stays in date order.

QWhat do the figures across the top mean?

Coupons is how many were used, Total amt is their total value and Avg / coupon is the average. Products and Sub depts count the different items and sub departments. Vendor is how many were vendor coupons.

QWhat are the Sub dept, Date and Cashier tabs?

They group the same coupons in different ways. Click a group to see only its coupons and totals, and click it again to go back to everything.

QWhat does the product table show?

Each row is a product, with how many transactions it was used in (Trans), how many coupons (Count) and the total amount. Click a row to list each use with the cashier, date, transaction ID, quantity and amount.

QHow do I see the whole transaction?

Click one of the uses under a product. You'll see every line on that sale, marked Sale, Cpn or Tender, with coupons, tax, net and total at the bottom. Click Back to coupons to return, or the download icon to export it.

QCan I filter the product table?

Yes. Click the UPC, Product, Sub dept or Total heading to filter. The Total filter lets you show amounts greater than, less than or equal to a dollar figure.

QHow do I export coupons?

Click the download icon at the top right to open Export CSV. Presets downloads the coupon records for what you're viewing, and lets you pick stores for a group. Custom lets you choose how to group the data and which totals to include.

QHow do I start a new search?

Click the magnifying glass at the top of the store list to change the store, group or dates.
