Orders

Find your stores' orders by type and date, then open any order to see its line items with cost, retail and profit.

Common questions
QHow do I find orders?

Choose Store or Group, pick the store or group, set the date range and click Find orders. The button says "Select Store" or "Select Group" until you pick one, so if it won't click, check that first.

QWhat does the Available Orders list show?

Each store and date that has orders, with how many orders it has. Use the tabs to see all order types or just one. On the All tab, click an order type to open or close it.

QHow do I narrow the list?

Type a store name or number in the filter box, or pick a date from the "All dates" list.

QWhat does "Select all stores" do?

It shows up when you're on one order type tab and more than one store is listed. Click it to see that order type for all the listed stores together. If you picked a date, it covers that day only. If not, it covers your whole date range.

QHow do I open an order and see its lines?

Click a store row and its orders appear as cards showing the order number, status, invoice total, ext cost, item count and sub departments. Click an order to see its line items on the right, with the order's totals across the top.

QCan I filter by sub department, UPC or description?

Use the "All sub depts" list above the order cards to show only orders and totals for one sub department. In the line items, click the UPC, Description or Sub dept heading to filter those lines.

QWhat do Cost, Ext cost, Ext retail, Profit and Margin mean?

Cost is the cost per unit, not the case cost. Ext cost is that unit cost times the quantity, and Ext retail is the retail price times the quantity. Items sold by weight use the weight instead. Profit is Ext retail minus Ext cost, and Margin is profit as a percent of Ext retail.

QHow do I export orders?

Once you've picked a store, click the download icon at the top right to open Export CSV. Presets lets you download all orders, the selected order, or chosen sub departments. Custom lets you pick the columns and totals yourself.

QHow do I start a new search?

Click the magnifying glass at the top of the Available Orders list to change the store, group or dates.
