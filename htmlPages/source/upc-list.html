UPC List

Study a list of UPCs across a store or group and date range: sales against last year, price history, trends, and what sells alongside them.

Common questions
QHow do I set up a search?

Choose the tab to open to, pick Store or Group, and set your dates. Paste your UPCs and click Add. They only join the list once you click Add. Then click Search. The button reads "Select Store" or "Select Group" until you've picked one, and it stays off until you've added at least one UPC.

QCan I upload a list of UPCs?

Yes. Click Upload CSV and choose a .csv or .txt file with UPCs separated by commas, spaces or new lines. Only numbers are picked up. To remove one UPC, click the × on it, or click Clear all to start over.

QWhy does it say "No UPCs selected"?

After a search, tick the items you want in the left panel, or click All to tick every one. The tabs and totals only cover ticked items. Use the Filter box to find an item quickly.

QWhat does Sales Comp show?

It shows sales for the ticked items this year compared with the same weeks last year. WoW trend compares each item's most recent full week with its average full week. Click an item in the list to see its sales day by day for each week.

QWhat does Price Opt show?

It lists every price each item sold at in your date range, with the quantity and revenue at each price. The price marked "best" brought in the most revenue. Elasticity shows how much sales moved when the price changed, and an item needs at least two prices to show it.

QWhat does Trend show?

It compares each item's daily sales before and after a point in your range and labels it Growing, Declining, Accelerating or Reduced availability, with the biggest losses first. Reduced availability means the daily rate held up but the item sold on fewer days, which often points to a stocking problem. Confidence shows how steady the pattern is.

QWhat does Association show?

It shows what else was bought in the same baskets as your ticked items. Attach is the share of those baskets that also held the product. Click a product to see what sells with it, then click Back to Seed UPCs to return.

QHow do I change my search?

Click the magnifying glass at the top of the left panel. You can add or remove UPCs, or change the store, group or dates, then click Search.

QCan I export the results?

Yes. Click the download icon at the top right. It exports the tab you're on as a CSV file, with options such as all UPCs or only the ones you've ticked.
