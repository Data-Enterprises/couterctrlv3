Loss Prevention

Spot stores and cashiers with unusual exception activity, such as voids, refunds and no-sales, so you can look into it early.

Common questions
QWhat does this page show?

Exception activity for one week, store by store, compared with each store's own prior two weeks. Pick a store to see its cashiers and the transactions behind the numbers.

QHow do I run a search?

Choose a store or group and a week ending date, then click Load exceptions. The week is the seven days ending on that date. To search again, click the magnifying glass at the top of the store list.

QHow do I pick an exception type?

Click a type in the row of tabs above the store list. The page opens on one type for you, and switching type reloads the stores for the one you picked.

QWhat is the baseline?

The same store's (or cashier's) average for one week over the prior two weeks. Everything is compared with its own history, not with other stores or cashiers.

QWhat do Critical, Watch and OK mean?

They show how many figures (transactions, quantity, total and average) have gone up against the baseline. Critical means most of them rose, Watch means about half did, and OK means most held at or below it. Red numbers are above the baseline and green are at or below it; a grey dot on a cashier means there is no history to compare yet.

QHow do I see a store's cashiers and transactions?

Click a store in the list. The right side shows its totals against the baseline, its cashiers and its transactions. Click a cashier to see only their transactions, and click them again to show everyone.

QHow do I look at a single day?

Use the Whole week drop-down above the store list to narrow the stores to one day. Inside a store, click a day card to narrow the cashiers and transactions to that day, or click All Week to go back. A single day is compared with the same weekday in the prior two weeks.

QHow do I open a receipt?

Click the transaction ID in the transaction list. Click Back to return to the list.

QHow do I sort or filter the lists?

Click a column heading in the store list to sort by it; click again to reverse it, and a third time to go back to worst first. Type in Filter by store to narrow the list, or click Crit, Watch or OK to show only those. The transaction list can be filtered by clicking Trans ID, Date, Qty or Total.

QCan I export the data?

Yes. With a store open, click the download icon in its title bar to save its transactions or cashiers as a CSV file.
