Receivers

See a store's receiving history by date and invoice, and exactly what was received on each invoice.

Common questions
QHow do I load receivers?

Pick a store, set the date range and click Load Receivers. This page works one store at a time. The button says "Select Store" until you pick one.

QWhat does the list show?

Receiving dates, newest first, with the number of invoices on each. Click a date to see its invoices with the invoice number, item count and vendor. Total at the top is the number of invoices loaded.

QCan I filter by vendor or date?

Yes. Use the "All Vendors" and "All Dates" lists above the list. Matching dates open for you while a filter is on.

QWhy do I see location tabs?

Some stores have more than one location. When they do, tabs appear above the filters so you can switch between locations.

QHow do I see what was on an invoice?

Click the invoice in the list. The right side shows the vendor, who entered it, the date, the invoice and Ref numbers, the totals for the invoice and every line received.

QWhat do the columns mean?

Cases and Units are what was received. U Cost is the cost per unit and Ext Cost is the total cost for the line. Retail and Ext Retail show the same for the retail price, and the Totals row at the bottom adds up the invoice.

QHow do I export an invoice?

Click the download icon in the invoice header once its lines load. Presets downloads all the line items. Custom lets you group by UPC or description and pick the figures and totals you want.

QHow do I start a new search?

Click the magnifying glass at the top of the list to change the store or dates. If nothing is found, you'll see a notice with a Search again button.
