Common questions
QHow do I set up a search?

Choose the tab to open to, pick Store or Group, and set your dates. Paste your UPCs and click Add. They only join the list once you click Add. Then click Search. The button reads "Select Store" or "Select Group" until you've picked one, and it stays off until you've added at least one UPC.

QCan I upload a list of UPCs?

Yes. Click Upload CSV and choose a .csv or .txt file with UPCs separated by commas, spaces or new lines. Only numbers are picked up. To remove one UPC, click the × on it, or click Clear all to start over.

QWhy does it say "No UPCs selected"?

After a search, tick the items you want in the left panel, or click All to tick every one. The tabs and totals only cover ticked items. Use the Filter box to find an item quickly.

QWhat does Sales Comp show?

It shows sales for the ticked items this year compared with the same weeks last year. WoW trend compares each item's most recent full week with its average full week. Click an item in the list to see its sales day by day for each week.

QWhat does Price Opt show?

It lists every price each item sold at in your date range, with the quantity and revenue at each price. The price marked “best” is the one that brought in the most profit once cost is known, which isn’t always the one with the most revenue. Elasticity shows how much sales moved when the price changed, and an item needs at least two prices to show it.

Items sold by weight are priced per pound, so their best price reads /lb and the Volume at best tile shows pounds. Other items show Qty at best.

QWhat does Trend show?

It compares each item's daily sales before and after a point in your range and labels it Growing, Declining, Accelerating, Reduced availability or New since pivot, with the biggest losses first.

Accelerating means the decline is getting steeper, not easing. Reduced availability means the daily rate held up but the item sold on fewer days, which often points to a stocking problem. New since pivot means the item had no sales before that point, so there is nothing to compare it with. Confidence shows how steady the pattern is.

QWhat does Association show?

It shows what else was bought in the same baskets as your ticked items. Attach is the share of those baskets that also held the product. Click a product to see what sells with it, then click Back to Seed UPCs to return.

QHow do I change my search?

Click the magnifying glass at the top of the left panel. You can add or remove UPCs, or change the store, group or dates, then click Search.

QCan I export the results?

Yes. Click the download icon at the top right. It exports the tab you're on as a CSV file, with options such as all UPCs or only the ones you've ticked. A Unit column says whether each row counts units or pounds, so a mix of weighed and packaged items still adds up correctly.

QA tab didn't load. What do I do?

If a tab couldn't get its data, open it again or change your search and it will try again. A failed load is no longer remembered as “no results”.

What the terms mean

Sales Comp

Compares this year's sales to last year and recent weeks, to catch items underperforming their own history.

Peak day
The day generating the most dollars across your selection — not just the most common peak day per item, those can disagree, and we show both.
Avg daily / UPC
Average per active-selling day; days with zero sales aren't counted.
WoW trend
Compares each item's most recent complete week against the average of all complete weeks in range, not last week vs. the week before.
Heatmap
Cell color reflects average daily sales for that day-of-week.
  • Global scaleColored against the highest day-value across your entire selection, so colors are comparable row to row.
  • Per item scaleColored against each UPC's own peak day, so its own weekly pattern shows clearly regardless of size.
LY row
Shown when a product's expanded — same calendar weeks, one year back.

Price Opt

Checks whether an item's current price is actually costing you profit, based on what price performed best historically.

Best price
The price point that generated the most total profit historically once your cost is known — not necessarily the one with the most revenue.
Profit at risk
Estimated dollars left on the table by staying at the current price instead of the best one, using historical quantity at each price as the estimate.
Units suppressed
Estimated units lost to being priced above the best price.
Elasticity
How much demand shifts with price; more negative means more price-sensitive.
Group searches
Show historical pricing only until you pick a store — needed to know your cost.

Trend

Flags items whose daily sales pattern is meaningfully shifting, and tries to tell you whether that's really about demand or just about the item not being stocked.

Reduced availability
Total volume dropped, but the daily rate held or improved — usually a stocking issue, not weaker demand.
Declining
Total volume and daily rate both dropped — a real demand decline.
Accelerating
A declining item where the rate of decline is getting worse.
Confidence
How reliable the trend estimate is; higher means the pattern is consistent, not noise.

Association

Shows what else gets bought alongside your selected items, for cross-merchandising or understanding a purchase pattern.

Seed UPCs
The checked items feeding the search; uncheck any to narrow the basket set and refetch.
Attach rate
The share of baskets containing your seed items that also contained this product.
Exploring
Click any companion to re-root the panel around it and see its own associations; also reachable by right-clicking any item anywhere. The back link returns to your seed view instantly, no refetch.
