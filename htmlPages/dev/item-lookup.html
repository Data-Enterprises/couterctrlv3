Item Lookup

Look up specific UPCs at a store to see how they're actually performing — margin, pricing, and sales gaps — without digging through the full sales report.

Common questions
QHow do I look up items?

Pick a store, then type or paste one or more UPCs, separated by commas or new lines. Click Search. The button stays off until you have picked a store and entered at least one UPC. To search again later, click the magnifying glass above the item list.

QWhat dates does it cover?

Always the last 14 days, ending yesterday, because today's sales are still coming in. The dates are shown at the top of the item list. You can't change the range on this page.

QWhat is the list on the left?

It shows every UPC you searched, with its margin once it has loaded. Each item loads on its own, so some may finish before others, and any item that can't be found shows a message. Click an item to see its full report on the right.

QWhat do Margin and Cost per unit mean?

Margin is the amount sold less the cost of goods over the 14 days at this store. Cost per unit is what one unit cost you after any vendor allowance, not the invoice list price. Avg sold at is the amount divided by what sold, so it drops below the list price when the item was on promotion for part of the time.

QWhy does it say "No cost on file"?

No cost has been set up for the item, so a real margin can't be worked out. The page says so instead of showing a false 100% margin. New items often sell before their cost is entered.

QWhy do some items show "lb" and a Weight column?

Those items are sold by weight. Their cost, average price and totals count pounds, not scans. In the daily breakdown, Qty is the number of times it was rung up and Weight is the pounds sold.

QWhat do the warnings and the Longest gap mean?

"Slowing down" means the second week sold fewer units than the first. "No sales in the last X days" means the item has not sold for two or more days up to yesterday, which can mean it is out of stock. Longest gap is the longest earlier stretch of days with no sales.

QWhat is "By sale type"?

It splits the item's register lines into Sale, Backup (restored transactions), Cancelled and Voided, each shown as a share of sales. Everything else on the report counts Sale lines only. Click a type to see its days in the daily breakdown, then click Back to sales to return.

QMy store has two locations. How do I see just one?

Tabs appear above the item list, one for each location plus Both. Click a tab to switch, and every figure updates to match.

QCan I export the results?

Yes. Click the download icon at the top of the report. You can choose a summary of all items, the daily breakdown for the item you're viewing, or all items by sale type. You can also pick your own columns. The file downloads as a CSV.

What the terms mean
Margin %
Amount less cost of goods, calculated fresh for this store over the last 14 days. Not carried over from wherever the UPC was copied from, so the same item can show different margins at different stores.
Cost / unit
What one unit cost you after any vendor allowance — not the list price on the invoice. An item on a vendor deal costs less than its invoice says, and this reflects that, which is why margin here matches Sub Dept Margins.
Cost / lb
The same figure on an item sold by weight. When you see "lb" instead of "unit", the item is priced on the scale and everything on this report counts pounds rather than scans.
Units vs. scans
On a scale item one scan can be several pounds — bananas ring about 2 lb at a time. Cost and margin count what was actually sold, not how many times the item was rung up. The daily breakdown shows both: Qty is the scans, Weight is the pounds.
Sale types
Every register line is a Sale, Backup (a transaction that was restored), Cancelled or Voided. Amount, margin, days sold and the daily breakdown count Sale lines only; the others are listed under By sale type, each as a share of sales.
Sale type timeline
Select a sale type to see its days in the daily breakdown. Cost and margin are left off for Backup, Cancelled and Voided, since they aren't sales. Select it again, or Back to sales, to return.
Avg sold at
Amount divided by what actually sold, so it stays comparable to the shelf price. Sits below it when the item spent part of the window on promotion.
Queue vs. Report
The queue (left) is your batch list and shows each item's status as it loads. The report (right) is the full 14-day detail for whichever one item you've selected.
14-day window
Ends yesterday, not today, since today's sales aren't finished posting yet.
Longest gap
The longest stretch of consecutive days with zero sales inside the 14-day window. A useful sign an item may be out of stock or delisted rather than just slow.
Slowing trend
Flags when the second week of the window sold noticeably fewer units than the first week, even if the item is still selling.
Loading status (queue)
Each queued UPC fetches independently, so some can finish while others are still loading or error out.
