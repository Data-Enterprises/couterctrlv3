Vendors

Shows which of a store's suppliers are falling behind, then lets you drill into the items and departments where it happened.

Common questions
QWhat does this page show?

Every vendor that sold in the store that week, with this year (TY) against last week (vs LW) and last year (vs LY). The list puts the weakest first; click the TY, vs LW or vs LY heading to sort, and click a third time to go back.

QHow do I pick a store and week?

Choose a store and a week-ending date, then click Load vendors. You get the seven days ending on that date. To change either later, click the magnifying glass next to the store name.

QWhat does the Margin / Sales switch do?

It switches the whole page between gross margin and net sales, including the grades for vendors and items. Margin is sales minus cost of goods, as a percentage of sales; margin changes are shown in points (pts) and sales changes in percent.

QWhat do Crit, Watch and OK mean?

Each vendor is graded against last year, or against last week if last year is missing days. Crit means down by more than your Threshold, Watch means down but not past it, and OK means flat or up. Click a chip to show only those vendors, click it again to show all; a grey dot means there was no full week to grade on.

QWhy are some percentages grey, or say “3 of 7 days matched”?

Last week or last year is missing some days of this week, so only the matching days are compared. The figure still shows, but it is grey and does not decide the grade, because a part week can’t fairly mark a vendor as Crit.

QHow do I see a vendor and its items?

Click it in the list. The right side shows its week against last week and last year, a day strip, and every item it supplied; click an item to see its sales, contribution, margin and quantity, plus each weekday.

QHow do I look at just one day?

Click a day in the strip of day cards. The figures and items below narrow to that day; click the day again, or All Week, to go back to the full week.

QWhat is the “booked without a vendor” line?

Coupon lines and anything else the register recorded with no supplier. It is kept in the store total so the vendors still add up, but it isn’t a real vendor.

QCan I export this?

Yes. Click the download icon in the vendor’s title bar to save a CSV of vendors or items, or your own choice of columns. You can export the whole week or just the days you pick.

QWhat is the See item actions button?

In dev mode, Owners and above see a clipboard button in a vendor's report, with a count of the items that need attention. It opens Item Actions, which gathers the items behind the drop into one list to work through. Item Actions is still Coming Soon, so the page it opens is a preview.

What the terms mean
Crit / Watch / OK
Graded against last year, but only when last year has every day of the week. If it's missing days, the grade uses last week instead. Crit means down more than your threshold; Watch means down, but not past it; OK means flat or up. Click a chip to show only those; click it again to clear.
Ungraded
Neither last week nor last year has every day of the week, or neither has a figure at all, so there's no full-week comparison to grade on. Shown with a grey dot and not counted under Crit, Watch or OK. The chip only appears when some exist.
Grey percentages
That comparison is missing days — for example, last year only has 3 of the 7 days. The number is still shown, but it isn't used for the grade, because a partial week can't fairly call a vendor Crit. The header pills go grey the same way and show how many days matched.
Threshold
How far a decline has to go before a vendor is flagged Crit — points in margin mode, percent in sales mode. The Items tab has its own separate threshold, since a single UPC moving is a smaller thing than a whole vendor moving.
No vendor
Coupon (CPN) lines and anything else the POS books without a supplier. Kept in the list rather than dropped, so the vendors still add up to the store total — but it isn't a supplier, and a large bucket here is worth a look on its own.
Day strip
Picking a day narrows everything below it — the KPIs, the items, the departments — to that day alone. Click the selected day again, or All Week, to go back to the full week.
Items
Every UPC this vendor supplied, graded the same way the vendors are. Selecting one shows its margin, contribution and day-of-week pattern. Department-level breakdowns live on Sub Dept Margins rather than being repeated here.
Margin vs Sales
Swaps the whole page between gross margin and dollars, including every KPI and every grade — vendors and items alike. Margin moves are shown in POINTS, sales moves in percent; a two-point margin change is not a two percent change.
vs LW / vs LY
Only compares days present in both periods, so a week still in progress isn't dragged down by days that haven't happened yet. A dash means there's no comparable period at all.
Net sales and margin
Sales are net of tax and margin uses the same cost helper as Sub Dept Margins — net_cost before list, weight before scan count — so a vendor reconciles with the departments it sits in. The store figure in the header is weighted: dollars over dollars, not an average of the vendors' percentages.
Where the data comes from
Vendor is carried on the item rows, which arrive one department at a time. The page asks which departments sold this week, then walks all of them across three periods — which is why it takes longer to load than the pages above it.
Export
Covers whatever the date pills are set to, so a single day exports that day. Vendors are always the full list; items cover the open vendor only.
