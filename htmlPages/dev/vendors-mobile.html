Vendors

Margin for one store's week against the same week last year, by vendor. Open one to see its figures, then its items, then a single item's week day by day. Nothing is graded: the two years sit side by side and you draw the conclusion.

Common questions
QWhat does this page show?

The store’s week at the top, then a card for each vendor, biggest sellers first. Last year (LY) is the same weekdays last year, so a Monday is compared with a Monday. Tap the search icon on the store card to change the store or week.

QWhat do TY margin, LY margin, gross profit and cost mean?

Margin is sales minus cost of goods, as a percentage of sales, this year (TY) and last year (LY); GPM on a row is the same figure. Gross profit is sales before tax minus that cost, and cost is what was actually paid after vendor allowances.

QWhat do the bars show?

Sales this year against last year, on the cards, the day chart and every vendor and item. The percentage on a card is how much its sales changed from last year.

QWhat do “no history” and “days matched” mean?

“No history” means there is no record for this store last year, not that it sold nothing. “Last year: 3 of 7 days matched” means only some days line up, so only those days are compared.

QHow do I open a vendor and see its items?

Tap its card to open it. Tap a day in its chart to see just that day, and tap it again for the full week. Tap View items for its item list, then tap an item to see its week day by day.

QHow do I find an item or scan a barcode?

Tap Find an item or scan to search the whole store by name or UPC, or tap the scan button to read a barcode. Inside a vendor’s item list, the box and scan button search just that list; if nothing turns up, tap the option to search the whole store.

QHow do I sort the list?

Tap Sales, Profit, GPM, Change vs LY or Name. Tap the same one again to reverse it, and once more to put the list back.

QMy store ID covers two locations. How do I see each one?

Location buttons appear at the top of the page. Tap a store to see it; the two are never added together.

What the terms mean
The card at the top
The store you searched, for the whole week — its margin, and its sales against last year. It doesn't open, because what is inside it is the list of vendors underneath.
TY margin / LY margin
Gross margin this year: sales minus cost of goods, as a percentage of sales. LY margin is the same for last year.
Bars
Sales this year (TY) against last year (LY) — on the cards, the day chart and every vendor and item in a list. The percentage says how healthy; the bars say how much was sold.
Gross profit / Cost
Sales before tax minus the cost of goods sold, and that cost — this year on the top row, last year beneath. Cost is what was actually paid after vendor allowances, and weighted items cost by the pound.
TY / LY
LY is the same weekdays last year, matched day by day, so a Monday is compared with a Monday. "No history" means we have no record for this store last year — not that it sold nothing.
Open a vendor
Tap it to open its report where it sits. One opens at a time; the others shrink to a single line so the one you opened has the screen.
Tap a day
Inside an open vendor, scopes that vendor to the day — its figures and its items. It belongs to that card only, and clears when you open a different one. Tap the same day again for the full week.
View items
Opens that vendor's items on their own screen. Tap an item to see its week day by day. Back returns to the list with your card still open.
Find an item or scan
Searches every item in the store's week, not just one vendor — by name, by UPC, or by scanning a barcode. Each result names the vendor it came from, and tapping one goes straight to that item's week.
Filtering and scanning a list
The box above a list narrows it to what you type, and the scan button fills it from a barcode. Inside a vendor that only covers its own items, so if a scan finds nothing the screen offers to search the whole store for it instead.
Sort by
Sales puts the biggest sellers first. Profit puts the biggest earners first. GPM puts the thinnest margins first. Change vs LY puts the biggest drop first; anything with no sales last year goes to the bottom. Name sorts A to Z. Tap the same one again to reverse it, and once more to put the list back the way it arrived.
Each page keeps its place
Sub Dept Margins, Vendors and Categories each remember their own search, sort and open card. Moving between them leaves the others as you left them.
Location
A few stores share one store ID across two locations. When that happens you can switch between them; the figures are never added together.
