3. Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored by an internet browser on a computer or device.

Purposes

  • Authenticate users
  • Maintain secure sessions
  • Remember preferences
  • Understand how the Services are used
  • Measure performance
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Improve functionality
  • Protect against unauthorized activity

Cookie types

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the core operation and security of the Services. They may support authentication, account access, session management, security, and access to protected areas.

Performance and Functionality Cookies

These cookies help improve the performance and functionality of the Services. They may remember user preferences and previously selected settings.

Analytics and Customization Cookies

These cookies help us understand how the Services are accessed and used.

  • Page URL
  • Page title
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Language
  • IP address
  • Interaction information
  • Application performance information

You may configure your browser to reject or remove certain cookies. Some Services or features may not function correctly when cookies are disabled. Essential cookies cannot be rejected because they are necessary to provide secure access to the Services.

4. Do Not Track

Some browsers and devices offer a “Do Not Track” setting that signals a preference regarding online tracking.

There is currently no universally accepted standard for responding to these signals. At this time, our Services do not respond to Do Not Track browser signals.

If applicable laws require us to recognize a particular tracking preference signal, we will update our practices and this Privacy Policy accordingly.
