Getting Started March 13, 2026 CounterCtrl Cloud

How Onboarding Actually Works

If the number on our screen doesn't match the number in your office, nothing else we build matters.

Getting started with CounterCtrl Cloud follows the same sequence for every new location, and the sequence is deliberately front-loaded toward trust rather than features.

First, we connect to the data an operator already produces — POS, ERP, inventory, DSD receiving. No new hardware, no rip-and-replace of systems that already work. The goal at this stage is purely to get the existing data flowing into CounterCtrl without disrupting how the store actually operates day to day.

Second, and this is the step most vendors skip, we validate our numbers against the customer's existing numbers until they agree. Every store has a set of figures its team already trusts — last month's sales, last week's shrink, this quarter's variance. Before anything gets rolled out to the floor, we make sure CounterCtrl produces the same figures those teams already believe. If our number and their number don't match, we don't move forward until we understand why and fix it.

Third, we train the team on the screens they'll actually use day to day — not a full manual walkthrough of every feature, but the handful of views a store director or an LP lead will open every single morning. The goal is competence on Monday, not familiarity with a feature list.

Fourth, we keep tuning it with the customer after go-live, because the first version of any rollout is rarely the last word on what a specific team needs from it.

The reason step two exists at all is simple: if the number on the CounterCtrl screen doesn't match the number already sitting in a store director's head, nothing else about the platform — the comparisons, the pattern detection, the dashboards — will get trusted enough to actually change how anyone runs their week.
