Announcement March 10, 2026 CounterCtrl Cloud

CounterCtrl Cloud Is Live

Five modules, one source of truth, for regional grocery and retail operators.

CounterCtrl Cloud is live, built by DCR POS, a Data Enterprises company, as a retail intelligence platform for regional grocery and retail operators.

The platform is organized around five modules, all reading from a single consistent set of definitions.

Sales covers performance by store, department, and item, with this week / last week / last year comparison built into every view — so a trend is visible the moment it appears, rather than something a reader has to go calculate for themselves.

Loss Prevention analyzes register transactions — voids, refunds, no-sales, price overrides, cancelled transactions — at the item and cashier level, surfacing recurring exception patterns automatically instead of leaving a director to scan a raw exception list for something unusual.

Orders tracks what was ordered against what actually arrived, so receiving variance shows up as a number instead of an unpleasant surprise discovered later.

Weekly Performance takes the operating rhythm every grocery team already runs — the numbers pulled, the meeting held, the decisions made — and removes the assembly work that normally happens before the meeting can start.

Coupons reconciles promotional redemption against real unit movement, so a promotion's actual impact on the shelf is visible rather than assumed from redemption counts alone.

None of the five modules exist as separate tools stitched together after the fact. They share one data foundation, which is what makes a number in one screen trustworthy against a number in another.

CounterCtrl Cloud is in production with grocery operators today, with additional locations onboarding through the fall as part of a phased rollout built around validating our numbers against each customer's own before a single store goes fully live.
