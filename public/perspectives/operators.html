District · Regional · Store & Department Managers · Coordinators

If I am a store manager, what does CounterCtrl Cloud provide me?

Yesterday’s numbers, already graded, before the doors open — scoped to exactly what you are responsible for and nothing else. Closest to the shelf, shortest window to act on what you find.

If I cover a district or region

Your stores at a glance
Every location you cover, graded, so the one that is off pace is the one you open.
One click deeper
Store to category to item without leaving the screen or asking anyone to pull a report.

If I run a store or a department

Overnight sales, already graded
Yesterday by department, with last week and last year attached, ready before the shift starts.
Scoped to your aisle
A department manager sees their department. Permissions decide the reach, so nobody wades through someone else's numbers.

If I coordinate pricing, receiving or customer service

The item or the delivery you own
Look up a UPC, check what arrived against what was ordered, and see the answer on the spot.
Send it on
Whatever you land on can go to the person who needs it, as it is.
