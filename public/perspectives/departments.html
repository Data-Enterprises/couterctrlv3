Loss Prevention · Operations · Marketing · Pricing

If I am in a department, what does CounterCtrl Cloud provide me?

The answer to your own question, on your own schedule, without filing a request and waiting on somebody else to assemble it. Each department owns a different slice of the same nightly load — the screens differ, the definitions underneath them do not.

If I am in Loss Prevention

Register activity at item and cashier level
Voids, refunds, discounts and no-sales read as patterns rather than a list of every exception in the chain.
Ranked by exposure
The biggest problems surface first instead of the most frequent ones.

If I am in Operations

Ordered against arrived
Receiving and DSD variance shows up as a number the same night, not as a surprise found weeks later.
Store and department performance
Where the operating rhythm is holding and where it is not, location by location.

If I am in Marketing

Coupons against real movement
Redemption reconciled to actual unit movement, so a promotion's impact is measured instead of assumed.
Category and item response
What the promotion moved, where it moved, and what it moved instead.

If I am in Pricing

UPC Search & Analysis
Follow one item wherever it goes — movement, margin, price changes and promotions across every store.
Margin where it moved
See the price change and the unit response side by side rather than in two exports.
